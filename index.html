About Me (Your Local Tour Guide)

Hello and welcome! I am your dedicated local nature and cultural tour guide right here in Lushoto. Born and raised within the breathtaking ridges of the Usambara Mountains, I have a deep, lifelong connection to this land. Growing up here has given me an intimate, insider’s knowledge of every hidden trail, local history, Shambaa traditions, and the rich biodiversity and wildlife of our region.

My mission is to offer you an authentic, safe, and unforgettable Tanzanian experience directly—with fair, transparent pricing and No middleman fees! Plus, I ensure you capture wonderful memories with great photos and videos during your journey.
